Italy's Under 17 side has kicked off their World Cup campaign in emphatic fashion, dismantling Jamaica 6-0 at the Bader Bilal stadium in Doha's Aspire Zone. Daniele Franceschini's team produced a dominant display in Group E, combining attacking fluidity with defensive solidity throughout the contest.

Fiorentina's Federico Croci orchestrated the first-half onslaught with a brace: opening the scoring in the 7th minute before doubling his tally just eight minutes later, earning FIFA's Player of the Match award. Prior to the interval, Atalanta's striker Diego Perillo added a third, with Jamaica's Jahmarie Justin Kasheem Nolan—a playmaker for Toronto—bundling an own goal into the net to make it four.

The Italian side maintained their intensity after the break: Roma's midfielder Gioele Giammattei produced an acrobatic finish in the 57th minute, before Juventus winger Thomas Corigliano sealed victory at the 79th mark. A late strike saw Lazio's Jacopo Landi strike the woodwork as Italy brought the encounter to a commanding close.
