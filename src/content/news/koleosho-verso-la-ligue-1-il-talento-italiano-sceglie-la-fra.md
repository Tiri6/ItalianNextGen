Italian winger Luca Koleosho will not continue in Serie A. The young talent has secured a permanent move to Paris FC, opting for the French league instead of remaining in Italy's top division.

For one of the prospects monitored by Italian Next Gen, this represents an important opportunity to develop in an international environment. At Paris FC, Koleosho will have the chance to gain valuable experience away from the pressures of Serie A. The French challenge could prove decisive for his growth as part of the squad-building project toward the 2030 World Cup.
