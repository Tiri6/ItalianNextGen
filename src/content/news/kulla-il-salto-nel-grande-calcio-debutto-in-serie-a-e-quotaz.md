Gabriel Kulla ha compiuto il salto tanto atteso verso la massima serie italiana. L'esordio in Serie A con il Sassuolo rappresenta un momento cruciale nella carriera del giovane talento, che ha catturato l'attenzione degli addetti ai lavori con le sue prestazioni.

Gabriel Kulla has achieved the long-awaited leap to Italian top-flight football. His Serie A debut with Sassuolo marks a crucial milestone in the young talent's career, having impressed observers with his performances.

The player's market valuation has jumped to €1.4 million, a clear sign of the confidence placed in his qualities by the football establishment. The increase reflects both the expectations generated by his debut and the potential recognized by scouts and analysts in the sector.
