Carmine Baldini ha deciso di allargare la base della sua Under 21 inserendo quattro volti nuovi nella lista dei convocati per le gare contro Armenia e Polonia, impegni che rappresentano un passaggio importante nella preparazione verso il prossimo Europeo di categoria.

<!--EN-->

Carmine Baldini has decided to broaden his Under 21 squad by including four new faces in the call-up list for the matches against Armenia and Poland, commitments representing an important milestone in preparation for the next European Championship in the category.

Among the debutants are Matteo Cichella, Luca Reggiani and Mattia Liberali, three players in whom the coach places confidence for the continuation of the project. The choice reflects Baldini's intention to assess fresh resources and maintain internal competition within the Azzurri group.
