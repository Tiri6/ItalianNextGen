Italy's Under 19 team is preparing for the tournament in Croatia with a squad that includes several highly regarded prospects. The call-up list features Andrea Natali and Antonio Arena, two players earmarked for future development.

Notably absent are any players from AC Milan's youth ranks, a detail that underscores how the selection has been distributed across different academy systems throughout Italy.
