La Viola sembra aver accelerato i tempi nelle trattative, guadagnando terreno rispetto ai biancocelesti che ora vedono allontanarsi le chances di assicurarsi il classe 2003. La competizione tra club importanti per un prospetto di questa qualità testimonia il valore riconosciuto all'interno dell'ecosistema calcistico nazionale.

Giovanni Fabbian continues to be one of Italian football's most sought-after talents. Following Lazio's initial interest, Fiorentina has now made a concrete move for the midfielder, intensifying pressure around his future.

The Viola have accelerated their pursuit, gaining ground on the Biancocelesti, who risk losing their grip on the 2003-born prospect. The competitive interest from multiple prominent clubs underscores the significant value attributed to this young talent across Italian football.

Fabbian embodies the exact midfielder profile Italy is counting on to refresh its midfield for upcoming international competitions, starting with the 2030 World Cup.
