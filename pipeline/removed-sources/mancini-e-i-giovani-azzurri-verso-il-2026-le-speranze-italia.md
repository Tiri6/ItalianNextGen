Tra valutazioni tecniche e considerazioni economiche, la scelta dei convocati seguirà criteri rigorous, con attenzione sia al potenziale che alla concretezza delle prestazioni. The season leading up to the 2026 World Cup represents a crucial opportunity for young Italian talent. With Luciano Mancini at the helm of the national team, a new chapter opens in the search for reinforcements and alternatives to face international challenges. The technical plan of the Italian Federation necessarily passes through the inclusion of new forces, emerging from a football movement that continues to generate interesting prospects.

The analysis of available profiles shows how the Italian market is rich with options for the head coach. Between technical assessments and economic considerations, the choice of call-ups will follow rigorous criteria, with attention to both potential and concrete performance delivery. Building the squad for the world tournament therefore represents a challenge that calls upon the best talents of the peninsula, ready to write a new chapter in the Azzurri's history.
