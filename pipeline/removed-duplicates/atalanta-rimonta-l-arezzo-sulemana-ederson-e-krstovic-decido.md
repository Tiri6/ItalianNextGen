Atalanta completed a convincing comeback against Arezzo, securing a 3-2 victory. The away side had managed to take the lead, but the Bergamaschi reacted with determination and composure, turning the match around.

Three players proved decisive in the turnaround: Sulemana opened the comeback, followed by Ederson, with Krstovic sealing the win. A display of character that showcases the squad's ability to respond when facing adversity during the match.

A performance that supports the project of building roster depth for upcoming fixtures, with Atalanta continuing to develop its competitive potential for future campaigns.
